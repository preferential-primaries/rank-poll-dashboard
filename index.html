Instant-runoff tabulation (GOP nomination)

Ballots are counted from the ranked GOP lists in view. Each round, the candidate with the fewest votes is eliminated and their ballots transfer to the next ranked choice still standing. A candidate wins with strictly more than 50% of non-exhausted ballots. Ties for elimination are broken by fewest first-choice votes, then alphabetically. Candidates differ by poll: February offered 12 names, April 5, August 9 — mixed-poll runoffs pool all names on offer.
